Text-input focus — 4 alternatives to the 2px ring

All inputs below are real — click into them to feel each treatment. The change is one convention, applied in one place per surface stylesheet: text inputs and textareas get the new focus style; buttons, toggles, and rows KEEP the 2px edge ring (it is correct there and carries the a11y contract). Inputs show focus through their caret anyway, so a lighter treatment stays accessible.

1 — Hairline + soft halo

Border turns purple at 1px, plus a 3px halo at ~14% alpha. The Stripe/Linear treatment: present without shouting. Closest to today in spirit, half the visual weight.

2 — Underline energy (Carbon)

Focus lives only in a 2px bottom underline plus a whisper of background tint. Pure Carbon text-input grammar — matches the tab-active underline you already use. The quietest option that still has a clear "on" state.

3 — Left keyline (AI surface grammar)

A 2px inset keyline on the left edge — the same mark your AI code blocks, connected servers, and chat accents already carry. Focus becomes part of the family language instead of a generic ring.

4 — The border wakes up (minimal)

Inline bar (dark)
Chat composer (light)
Settings field
Nothing is added: the existing 1px border simply turns purple, the caret and text selection are already purple. Maximum calm; relies on the caret to carry focus (which it does — you are typing). Risk: hardest to spot for keyboard-only users tabbing through, so buttons keeping the full ring matters most here.
