Flow hierarchy — different looks

Same data, three genuinely different paradigms. Bigger text, depth without thin connector rails.

A · Containment cards
Depth = nested shaded panels, not lines. The container is a card; its fields are chips inside. Bold headers, comfortable sizes. No connectors to read.
B · Drill-down (master / detail)
One level at a time, like a phone app. A breadcrumb path up top; the current container's items as big tappable rows. A chevron means "has more" — tap to go deeper. Nothing nests on screen.
C · Pipeline
A flow-diagram look: a bold gradient spine with node dots for the chain (InputView → InputSet), then the fields fan out as chips. Connectors are the point, not noise.
